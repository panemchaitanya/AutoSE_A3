# ADR-021: Longer per-call provider patience for hosted free-tier runs

## Status

Accepted, 24 November 2026, 22:50 IST, late work. Supersedes the default
constants of ADR-018 only (3 retries, 30/60/120 s). ADR-018 is kept. Stage
checks, correction bounds, step cap, token budget and model identities are
unchanged.

## Context

After the 18:42 IST freeze the student asked for the assignment to be completed
in full, so a fresh issue series (#35 to #39) was started. Four consecutive hosted
runs on issue #37 failed at stage 1 with Gemini HTTP 503 "high demand" before any
stage check ran: runs 45428682833, 45433683809, 45437784982 and 45442883647
(the last on gemini-3.5-flash-lite for every role). In 45442883647 the call log
shows two successful calls, then four 503 attempts across 16:57:32 to 17:01:54 UTC
and no alternate tier, because primary and fallback were the same model.
Direct availability probes in the same hour returned 503 for several models and
200 for gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Separately, a daily free-tier request quota
(429, limit 20 per day on the gemini-3.5-flash family for the key used) was hit,
which is a different cause with its own evidence and is not claimed for the
503 runs.

## Decision

Raise the defaults to 6 retries with 30, 60, 120, 180, 240 and 300 second
backoff, about 15 minutes of tolerated continuous outage per call. Everything is
still bounded and still overridable through API_RETRIES and API_BACKOFF_SECONDS.
A call that outlasts it fails the stage exactly as before, with its failure
artifact. Waiting costs no tokens.

## Alternatives

- Keep ADR-018 constants and retrigger by hand: four identical failures showed
  this mostly restarts the same lottery.
- Unbounded waiting: hides a stuck run.
- Switch to a paid tier: not authorized, and a spending decision.

## Consequences

Runs that hit a spike of up to about 15 minutes can continue. A sustained outage
now takes longer to surface. This does not prove availability or any issue
outcome. Runs under these constants are a new harness identity (commit of the
merge of this change) and are reported apart from the 24 November frozen series.

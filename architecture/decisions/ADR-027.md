# ADR-027: Move to the fallback tier after consecutive 429s

## Status

Accepted for merge after run 18 ends, 25 November 2026, 08:05 IST, late work. Extends ADR-006, ADR-018, ADR-021 and
ADR-022. Validation checks,
step caps, token budgets, models and keys are unchanged.

## Context

Run 45512804566 (#39) logged 95 model calls (artifact autose-issue-39-requirements):
51 were HTTP 429 on gemini-3.5-flash-lite, 38 succeeded on it, 3 succeeded on the
fallback gemini-3-flash-preview, 2 were 503 and 1 was a transport error. Bursts of 3 to 7
fast calls were followed by 429s that lasted the whole 15.5 minute retry ladder
(30, 60, 120, 180, 240, 300 s). Only then did the call go to the fallback tier, which
answered immediately all three times. About 62 of the job's 90 minutes were spent in
429 retries and the job hit its timeout.

## Decision

In `generate`, after `RATE_LIMIT_SWITCH_AFTER` (default 2) consecutive 429 responses on a
tier that still has a fallback tier, move to the fallback tier immediately. The fallback
tier keeps its full retry ladder. 503 and transport errors keep the full ladder on the
primary tier. Both models were already configured (ADR-006, ADR-022).

## Consequences

New harness identity. Runs 14 to 18 are a different configuration and are reported as
such. Effect unproven until a run uses it. Unit tests cover the early switch, the
unchanged 503 ladder and the fallback tier not being cut short.
